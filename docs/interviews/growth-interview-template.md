# Engineer Growth & Development Interview Template

**Purpose**: Understand how engineers learn and grow (or don't), identify gaps in development support, and discover what investment would look like.

**Format**: 1-on-1 conversation, 30-40 minutes
**Tone**: Casual and conversational - this is a chat, not a performance review

______________________________________________________________________

## Before You Start

**Interviewer prep:**

- Know the person's level/tenure (shapes which questions to emphasize)
- Be prepared for this to surface frustrations - growth is often a sore spot
- Record the session (with permission) so you can focus on listening

**Opening script:**

> "Hey, thanks for taking the time. I'm trying to understand how engineers here learn and grow - what's working, what's not, and what we could do better. This isn't about evaluating you - it's about understanding the environment. Be as honest as you want."

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 1: Context (3 min)

1. **How long have you been here, and what's your current role/level?**

2. **Is this your first dev role, or have you worked elsewhere before?**

   - *If yes: How did that place handle engineer development? Training, mentoring, anything formal?*

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 2: How You've Grown (8 min)

*Goal: Understand what learning has actually happened and how*

3. **What skills have you developed most since you've been here?**

   - *Probe: How did that happen? Formal training, figured it out yourself, learned from someone?*

4. **Think of something you're significantly better at now than when you started. How did you get better at it?**

   - *Probe: Was that intentional or did it just happen?*
   - *Probe: Did anyone help you, or was it mostly solo?*

5. **Has anyone here actively helped you grow - formally or informally?**

   - *If yes: What did that look like? How did it start?*
   - *If no: Would you have wanted that? What stopped it from happening?*

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 3: What's Missing (8 min)

*Goal: Identify gaps between current state and what people need*

6. **What do you want to get better at that you haven't had the chance to develop?**

   - *Probe: What's gotten in the way?*
   - *Probe: Do you know how you'd develop that skill if you had the time/support?*

7. **When you've been stuck on something - technically or professionally - what do you do?**

   - *Probe: Is there someone you go to? How did that relationship form?*
   - *Probe: What happens when you can't figure it out?*

8. **What would "the company investing in your growth" actually look like to you?**

   - *Let them answer first, then probe if needed: Training? Mentorship? Time? Conference budget? Career conversations?*

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 4: Career Path (5 min)

*Goal: Understand if there's clarity on progression*

9. **Do you know what the next step in your career here looks like?**

   - *Probe: Is that clear to you? Written down anywhere?*
   - *Probe: Do you know what you'd need to do to get there?*

10. **Have you had conversations with your manager about your growth or career trajectory?**

    - *Probe: How often? What comes out of those conversations?*
    - *Probe: Do those conversations lead to anything concrete?*

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 5: For Senior Engineers (5 min)

*Skip if junior/mid - or ask about their observations instead*

11. **Do you mentor or help develop other engineers? Formally or informally?**

    - *If yes: How did that start? Is it recognized or expected?*
    - *If no: Is that something you'd want to do? What would make it possible?*

12. **What would you need to be a better mentor?**

    - *Probe: Time? Structure? Training on how to mentor? Recognition?*

13. **When you see a junior engineer struggling, what happens?**

    - *Probe: Do you step in? Does anyone? Is there a system for this?*

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 6: The System (5 min)

*Goal: Understand structural/cultural barriers*

14. **Is there any formal structure for engineer development here?** (Training programs, mentorship assignments, learning budgets, etc.)

    - *If yes: Have you used it? How well does it work?*
    - *If no: What do you think is the reason it doesn't exist?*

15. **Does the culture here encourage or discourage taking time to learn?**

    - *Probe: What signals that?*
    - *Probe: What happens if you take time to learn something instead of shipping?*

16. **If you could change one thing about how this place develops engineers, what would it be?**

______________________________________________________________________

## Section 7: Wrap-up (2 min)

17. **If someone joined tomorrow at your level, what would you tell them about growing their career here?**

18. **Anything else I should know?**

______________________________________________________________________

## Closing

> "This was really helpful. We're trying to figure out how to actually invest in engineers here, and understanding the current state is the first step. If anything else comes to mind, feel free to reach out."

## Tips for Interviewers

1. **This topic can be emotional.** People may feel frustrated, overlooked, or stuck. Let them vent - that's data.

2. **Watch for resignation.** "That's just how it is" signals learned helplessness and a cultural problem.

3. **Note the difference between levels.** Juniors may not know what they're missing. Seniors may have given up asking.

4. **Probe on "informal mentorship."** It often means "I got lucky" - that's not a system.

5. **Listen for time as a barrier.** "I'd love to learn/mentor but there's no time" is a prioritization/culture problem.

6. **Don't promise anything.** You're gathering data, not committing to solutions.
