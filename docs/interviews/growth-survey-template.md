# Engineer Growth & Development Survey

**Purpose**: Understand how engineers learn and grow, identify gaps in development support, and discover what investment would look like.

**Estimated time**: 8-10 minutes
**Anonymity**: All responses are anonymous. Be honest - that's what helps us most.

---

## Survey Introduction

> We're trying to understand how engineers here learn and grow - what's working, what's not, and what we could do better. This isn't about evaluating anyone - it's about understanding the environment so we can improve it.
>
> Your honest feedback matters.

## Survey Implementation Notes

### Distribution Tips

1. **Segment results by level** - Juniors and seniors will have very different perspectives
2. **Watch for tenure patterns** - Do long-tenured people feel more stuck or less?
3. **Keep anonymous** - Growth is sensitive; people won't be honest otherwise
4. **Set expectations** - "We're using this to figure out how to invest in engineers"

### Analysis Framework

**Key metrics to track:**
- How growth happens (Q3) - formal vs informal vs self-directed
- Mentorship coverage (Q4) - what % have had help?
- Intentionality of growth (Q5) - accidental growth = systemic gap
- Biggest barriers (Q7) - prioritization signal
- Career path clarity (Q9) - by level
- Manager conversation frequency and quality (Q10, Q11)
- Mentorship supply vs demand (Q12, Q13)
- Culture signals (Q15, Q16)

**Red flags to watch for:**
- High % selecting "I haven't developed much since joining" (Q3)
- High % selecting "No - I've mostly figured things out on my own" (Q4)
- "No time" as top barrier (Q7) = prioritization problem
- "No guidance" as top barrier (Q7) = mentorship gap
- Low scores on "Senior engineers actively help junior engineers grow" (Q15)
- "Frowned upon" responses to learning time (Q16)

**Cross-cuts to analyze:**
- Q4 (had mentor) vs Q5 (intentional growth) - does mentorship correlate with intentionality?
- Q9 (career clarity) by Q1 (level) - do seniors have more clarity?
- Q12 (is mentor) vs Q1 (level) - are seniors actually mentoring?
- Q7 (barriers) by Q2 (tenure) - do barriers change over time?

### Building the Case

To make the case for investment, look for:
1. **Quantified gaps:** "X% of engineers have had no mentorship support"
2. **Barrier patterns:** "The top barrier is Y, cited by Z% of respondents"
3. **Demand signal:** "X% want formal mentorship; only Y% have it"
4. **Culture indicators:** "Only X% agree that learning is valued as much as shipping"
5. **Retention risk:** Cross-reference Q15 "I can see a future here" with other answers
