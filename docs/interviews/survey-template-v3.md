# Developer Experience Survey

**Purpose**: Understand developer workflows, identify pain points, and discover opportunities to improve how we work.

**Estimated time**: 10-12 minutes
**Anonymity**: All responses are anonymous. Be honest - that's what helps us most.

---

## Survey Introduction

> We're trying to understand what's actually working and what's not in your day-to-day work. This isn't about evaluating anyone - it's about finding patterns and opportunities to make things better for everyone.
>
> Your honest feedback matters. There are no wrong answers.

## Survey Implementation Notes

### Analysis Framework

**Segment responses by:**
- Q3 (experience level) - First-job vs experienced engineers will have different baselines
- Q4 (comparison to other orgs) - Critical context for interpreting responses
- Q2 (tenure) - Newer vs longer-tenured may see things differently

**Key metrics to track:**

*Visibility & Awareness:*
- Q6: % who understand connection to goals
- Q7: % with low/no visibility into others' work
- Q8: How people learn about changes (ad-hoc vs structured)
- Q19: Clarity on code ownership
- Q20: Frequency of surprise cross-impacts

*Work Clarity:*
- Q5: Clarity on what to work on
- Q11: Requirements clarity
- Q12: When requirement gaps surface

*Code Review:*
- Q13: Cycle time
- Q14: Review concentration (bottleneck signal if "same person every time")
- Q15: Feature changes during review
- Q16: Who's allowed to review (gatekeeping signal)

*Structure Appetite:*
- Q24: Would structure help?
- Q25: What kind of structure

**Red flags to watch for:**
- High % with low visibility (Q7) = siloed organization
- High % discovering issues in review or production (Q12) = process gap
- Reviews concentrated on few people (Q14) = bottleneck + gatekeeping
- High % wanting to review but not allowed (Q16) = gatekeeping
- High % surprised by others' work (Q20) = no coordination
- High appetite for structure (Q24) but no mechanism = latent demand

**Cross-cuts to analyze:**
- Q3 (experience) vs Q4 (comparison) - do experienced people see it worse?
- Q3 (experience) vs Q24 (structure appetite) - do experienced people want more structure?
- Q16 (does reviews) vs Q3 (experience) - who's allowed to review?
- Q7 (visibility) vs Q20 (surprised) - correlation validation

### Building the Case

To make the case for structural improvements:
1. "X% have low or no visibility into what others are working on"
2. "X% have been surprised by another engineer's work affecting theirs"
3. "X% would benefit from more structure"
4. "The top request is Y" (from Q25)
5. Compare responses from experienced engineers (Q3) to first-timers
